## 📌 How to stay up‑to‑date (quick‑action checklist)

1. **Follow the official tourism accounts** – `@visitingjogja`, `@tby_official`, `@yogyakarta`. Turn on post‑notifications.  
2. **Enable notifications for community pages** you care about (e.g., `@polamanfest`, `@area_jogja`).  
3. **Bookmark the Dinas Pariwisata “Calendar of Events”** – refreshed weekly with new listings, including smaller sport‑tourism and cultural fairs: https://visitingjogja.jogjaprov.go.id/event-agenda-pariwisata-jogja/  
4. **Monitor X (Twitter) hashtags** for real‑time buzz: `#Polaman2026`, `#CherryPop2026`, `#Prawirotaman2026`, `#WJNC2026`, `#GamelanFestival2026`.  
5. **Search TikTok with the same hashtags** – creators post short clips of line‑ups, stage‑setup, crowd‑size previews.  
6. **Ticket purchase** – most events link to a dedicated `.my.id` or `fasticket.id` portal (e.g., `polfest.my.id` for Polaman, `fastticket.id` for CherryPop). Save those URLs now.  
7. **If you need a private group (WhatsApp, Discord, Facebook Group)**, reach out to the community account via DM; many organizers share invite links on request.
